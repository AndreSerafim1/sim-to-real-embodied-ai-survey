## Overview

This repository accompanies a scientific literature survey on simulation-to-reality transfer, generalization, and adaptation in embodied robotic agents.

The survey examines existing methodologies, compares their advantages and limitations, and identifies open research challenges associated with deploying learned behaviors in physical environments.

## Research Taxonomy

The literature is organized according to the five methodological categories presented in the survey.

| Category | Description |
|---|---|
| Domain Randomization | Training diversity and robustness to environmental uncertainty. |
| System Identification and Simulation Fidelity | Physical modeling and simulator calibration. |
| Adaptive Policies and Reinforcement Learning | Adaptation to changing physical conditions. |
| Representation Learning and Generalization | Transferable and task-relevant representations. |
| Foundation Models for Embodied Agents | Knowledge transfer across tasks, environments, and robotic platforms. |
