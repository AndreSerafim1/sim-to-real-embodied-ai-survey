# 6. Adaptive Policies and Reinforcement Learning

This section contains research on adaptive robotic control, reinforcement learning, and mechanisms that allow agents to respond to changing physical conditions.

## Kumar et al. (2022)

**Title:** Adapting Rapid Motor Adaptation for Bipedal Robots

**Publication:** IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS47612.2022.9981091

**Relevance:** Investigates rapid motor adaptation for bipedal locomotion through the estimation of environmental conditions from proprioceptive observations.

## Nagabandi et al. (2019)

**Title:** Learning to Adapt in Dynamic, Real-World Environments Through Meta-Reinforcement Learning

**Publication:** International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR)

**Relevance:** Investigates model-based meta-reinforcement learning to enable rapid adaptation to changing system dynamics during physical robotic deployment.

## Kaushik et al. (2020)

**Title:** Fast Online Adaptation in Robotics Through Meta-Learning Embeddings of Simulated Priors

**Publication:** IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS45743.2020.9341462

**Relevance:** Introduces a meta-learning approach that uses simulated prior experiences to support efficient online adaptation and recovery from unexpected physical conditions.

## Peng et al. (2018)

**Title:** Sim-to-Real Transfer of Robotic Control with Dynamics Randomization

**Publication:** IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)

**Relevance:** Demonstrates how recurrent policies trained under randomized dynamics can support adaptation to previously unseen physical conditions.
