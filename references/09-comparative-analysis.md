# 9. Comparative Analysis

This section contains references supporting the comparative evaluation of sim-to-real transfer methodologies, their trade-offs, and their evaluation criteria.

## Pitkevich and Makarov (2024)

**Title:** A Survey on Sim-to-Real Transfer Methods for Robotic Manipulation

**Publication:** IEEE International Symposium on Intelligent Systems and Informatics (SISY)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/SISY62279.2024.10737545

**Relevance:** Reviews sim-to-real transfer methodologies for robotic manipulation and discusses evaluation criteria, benchmarking, methodological limitations, and opportunities for combining existing approaches.

## Li et al. (2025)

**Title:** Evaluating Real-World Robot Manipulation Policies in Simulation

**Publication:** Proceedings of the Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL 2024), PMLR, Volume 270, 2025

**Publication Link:** https://proceedings.mlr.press/v270/li25c.html

**Relevance:** Investigates the use of simulation to evaluate real-world robotic manipulation policies and examines the relationship between simulated and physical performance.
