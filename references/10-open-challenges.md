# 10. Open Challenges and Future Directions

This section contains research supporting the discussion of unresolved challenges and future research directions in sim-to-real transfer and embodied robotic intelligence.

## Jaquier et al. (2025)

**Title:** Transfer Learning in Robotics: An Upcoming Breakthrough? A Review of Promises and Challenges

**Publication:** The International Journal of Robotics Research, Volume 44, Issue 3, Pages 465-485

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1177/02783649241273565

**Relevance:** Examines fundamental challenges in robotic transfer learning, including transfer across robots, tasks, and environments, physical grounding, abstraction levels, negative transfer, and standardized evaluation.
