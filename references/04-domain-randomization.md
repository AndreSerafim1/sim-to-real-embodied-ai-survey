# 4. Domain Randomization

This section contains research on introducing variability into simulated environments to improve robotic perception, robustness, and physical transferability.

## Tobin et al. (2017)

**Title:** Domain Randomization for Transferring Deep Neural Networks from Simulation to the Real World

**Publication:** IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)

**Relevance:** Demonstrates visual domain randomization for transferring object localization models from simulation to physical robotic systems.

## Zhao et al. (2020)

**Title:** Sim-to-Real Transfer in Deep Reinforcement Learning for Robotics: A Survey

**Publication:** IEEE Symposium Series on Computational Intelligence (SSCI)

**Relevance:** Reviews visual and dynamics randomization, zero-shot transfer, and limitations associated with selecting randomization distributions.

## Peng et al. (2018)

**Title:** Sim-to-Real Transfer of Robotic Control with Dynamics Randomization

**Publication:** IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)

**Relevance:** Investigates randomized physical dynamics and recurrent policies for transferring robotic manipulation controllers to physical hardware.

## Huber et al. (2024)

**Title:** Domain Randomization for Sim2Real Transfer of Automatically Generated Grasping Datasets

**Publication:** IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA57147.2024.10610677

**Relevance:** Investigates domain randomization as a mechanism for evaluating and optimizing the transferability of robotic grasping trajectories.
