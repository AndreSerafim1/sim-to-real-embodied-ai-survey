# 7. Representation Learning and Generalization

This section contains research on transferable representations, self-supervised learning, semantic features, and training data diversity for improving robotic generalization.

## Jeong et al. (2020)

**Title:** Self-Supervised Sim-to-Real Adaptation for Visual Robotic Manipulation

**Publication:** IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA40945.2020.9197326

**Relevance:** Investigates self-supervised adaptation of visual representations to reduce discrepancies between simulated and physical robotic environments.

## Chang et al. (2025)

**Title:** Generalizable Imitation Learning Through Pre-Trained Representations

**Publication:** IEEE International Conference on Robotics and Automation (ICRA)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ICRA55743.2025.11127800

**Relevance:** Investigates pretrained visual representations and semantic keypoints to improve imitation learning across different objects and manipulation tasks.

## Miao et al. (2025)

**Title:** Knowledge-Driven Imitation Learning: Enabling Generalization Across Diverse Conditions

**Publication:** IEEE/RSJ International Conference on Intelligent Robots and Systems (IROS)

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/IROS60139.2025.11246479

**Relevance:** Explores semantic keypoint representations and structural relationships between objects to improve robotic generalization and demonstration efficiency.

## Lin et al. (2025)

**Title:** Data Scaling Laws in Imitation Learning for Robotic Manipulation

**Publication:** International Conference on Learning Representations (ICLR)

**Publication Link:** https://proceedings.iclr.cc/paper_files/paper/2025/hash/88b7b2c896506daabc8d3fd587055167-Abstract-Conference.html

**Relevance:** Examines the relationship between training data diversity, demonstration quantity, and robotic manipulation performance under unfamiliar conditions.
