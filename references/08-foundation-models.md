# 8. Foundation Models for Embodied Agents

This section contains research on large-scale pretrained models, multimodal learning, and knowledge transfer across robotic embodiments and tasks.

## Gaba et al. (2026)

**Title:** A Comprehensive Review of Generative Physical Artificial Intelligence

**Publication:** IEEE Internet of Things Journal

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/JIOT.2026.3671268

**Relevance:** Reviews generative physical AI architectures, including robotic foundation models, vision-language-action models, diffusion policies, and world foundation models. Discusses their applications and limitations in physical environments.

## Yang et al. (2024)

**Title:** Pushing the Limits of Cross-Embodiment Learning for Manipulation and Navigation

**Publication:** Robotics: Science and Systems (RSS)

**Paper Link:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2402.19432

**Relevance:** Investigates knowledge transfer across heterogeneous robotic embodiments and task domains, including manipulation and navigation, through shared policy training.
