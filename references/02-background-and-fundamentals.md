# 2. Background and Fundamentals

This section contains references supporting the fundamental concepts of embodied intelligence, robotic learning, simulation-to-reality transfer, and the reality gap.

## Sun et al. (2024)

**Title:** A Comprehensive Survey on Embodied Intelligence: Advancements, Challenges, and Future Perspectives

**Publication:** CAAI Artificial Intelligence Research

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.26599/AIR.2024.9150042

**Relevance:** Introduces embodied intelligence, the perception-cognition-behavior framework, and the interaction between agents and their environments.

## Liu et al. (2025)

**Title:** Aligning Cyber Space With Physical World: A Comprehensive Survey on Embodied AI

**Publication:** IEEE/ASME Transactions on Mechatronics

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/TMECH.2025.3574943

**Relevance:** Provides an overview of embodied AI architectures, multimodal learning, simulation platforms, and sim-to-real adaptation.

## Salvato et al. (2021)

**Title:** Crossing the Reality Gap: A Survey on Sim-to-Real Transferability of Robot Controllers in Reinforcement Learning

**Publication:** IEEE Access

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1109/ACCESS.2021.3126658

**Relevance:** Establishes reinforcement learning fundamentals, the sim-to-real training pipeline, and approaches for addressing the reality gap.

## Aljalbout et al. (2026)

**Title:** The Reality Gap in Robotics: Challenges, Solutions, and Best Practices

**Publication:** Annual Review of Control, Robotics, and Autonomous Systems

**DOI:** https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-control-031924-100130

**Relevance:** Defines the reality gap and performance gap, identifies sources of simulation discrepancies, and discusses existing solutions and evaluation methods.
